# WO-2026-05-20 — FortifyOS Wealth Atlas Expansion

## Status
Proposed / Ready for build planning

## Objective
Expand FortifyOS from a personal finance command center into a broader financial resource for wealth building: personal finance defense, market intelligence, stock/sector opportunity education, alternative assets, and wealth architecture.

## Product Thesis
FortifyOS should help users answer four questions:

1. **Am I financially stable enough to invest?**
2. **Where are major market opportunities forming?**
3. **Which names, sectors, and assets are worth studying — and why?**
4. **How do I protect and transfer wealth once I build it?**

The system should remain educational, disciplined, and stage-aware. It should present strong paths to explore, not personalized financial/legal/tax advice.

## Core Pillars

### 1. Personal Finance Defense
Existing foundation:
- Dashboard
- CSV import / snapshots
- debt, BNPL, bills, runway, cash-flow discipline
- 7-stage financial journey

Goal:
- Keep this as the entry gate.
- FortifyOS should continue warning users when investing conflicts with debt/cash-flow priorities.

### 2. Market Opportunity Atlas
New umbrella concept for investable themes beyond AI.

Initial lanes:
- AI / automation
- Energy & power
- Defense / aerospace
- Healthcare / longevity
- Commodities / hard assets
- Bitcoin / digital scarcity
- Real estate / REITs / land
- Small caps / recovery plays
- Alternative assets / collectibles

Each lane should eventually include:
- thesis summary
- why it matters now
- top names to research
- ETF / broad exposure options
- bull case
- bear case
- key signals to watch
- who this lane is appropriate for
- stage/risk suitability

### 3. Alternative Assets & Collectibles
Add an educational lane for non-traditional wealth categories.

Candidate categories:
- trading cards / graded cards
- watches
- art
- sneakers
- graded video games
- rare books
- memorabilia
- precious metals / tangible stores of value

Teaching concepts:
- scarcity vs demand
- condition and grading
- liquidity risk
- provenance
- hype cycles
- storage / insurance
- bid-ask spreads
- why rare does not always mean valuable

### 4. Wealth Architecture
Add education around protecting and transferring wealth.

Initial modules:
- trusts and estate planning basics
- revocable vs irrevocable trust concepts
- beneficiary hygiene
- power of attorney / healthcare directive awareness
- life insurance basics
- term vs permanent life insurance
- asset protection concepts
- tax-aware account positioning

Important constraint:
- This must be educational only. Trusts, insurance, estate, and tax topics vary by jurisdiction and require professional advice.

## Homepage Direction
Update homepage positioning from:
> personal finance tracking / debt command

To:
> FortifyOS helps users defend cash flow, understand markets, study wealth opportunities, and build structures that protect what they create.

Homepage should teach the expanded system in a clear path:

1. **Defend** — budget, debt, cash flow, emergency runway
2. **Read the battlefield** — macro radar, market conditions, signals
3. **Study opportunities** — AI portfolio, Bitcoin, sector/asset atlas
4. **Build architecture** — trusts, insurance, estate, taxes, asset protection
5. **Move by stage** — avoid chasing wealth lanes before the foundation supports them

## First Build Candidate
Recommended first implementation sequence:

1. Update homepage copy and sections to reflect the expanded FortifyOS mission.
2. Introduce a visible “Wealth Atlas” or “Opportunity Atlas” section/card on the homepage.
3. Add placeholder atlas lanes without full pages yet.
4. Position AI Portfolio and Bitcoin as completed / active atlas modules.
5. Add “Coming next” lanes: Energy, Defense, Healthcare, Commodities, Real Estate, Alternatives, Wealth Architecture.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Homepage no longer reads as only a CSV/debt tracker.
- Users can understand the full FortifyOS roadmap within 60 seconds.
- AI Portfolio and Bitcoin feel like part of a broader wealth system, not isolated pages.
- Alternative assets and wealth architecture are explicitly represented.
- Copy preserves FortifyOS discipline: educational, stage-aware, no hype, no personalized financial/legal/tax advice.

## Non-Goals for First Pass
- Do not build all sector pages at once.
- Do not add personalized investment recommendations.
- Do not add legal/tax document generation.
- Do not claim FortifyOS replaces a financial advisor, attorney, CPA, or insurance professional.

## Notes
This WO came from the product direction discussion on 2026-05-20: FortifyOS should become a financial resource for investing and wealth architecture, with stock-pick style research paths similar to the AI Portfolio, plus alternative assets and legacy planning education.
